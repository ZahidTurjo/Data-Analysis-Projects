An interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer segments, regions, categories, and shipping modes.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that provides meaningful business insights.

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- What is the total sales and profit?
- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Which products have the highest sales?
- Which category contributes the most to sales?
- Which cities generate the highest revenue?
- How are sales distributed across customer segments?
- Which shipping mode is most frequently used?
- How do sales change month by month?
- What is the daily sales trend?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by City
Shows sales performance across different cities.

### 2. Sales by Region
Compares sales across:

- Central
- East
- South
- West

### 3. Sales by Customer Segment
Analyzes sales contribution from:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 4. Top 10 Products by Sales
Displays the top-performing products based on sales.

### 5. Sales by Ship Mode
Analyzes sales distribution across:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day

### 6. Quantity by Category
Shows the percentage contribution of different product categories.

### 7. Monthly Sales
Visualizes monthly sales trends throughout the year.

### 8. Sales by Category
Shows category-level sales contribution using a donut chart.

### 9. Daily Sales Trend
Shows how sales fluctuate throughout the month.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Some important insights from the dashboard include:

- The **East region** generates the highest sales among the four regions.
- **Consumer customers** contribute the largest share of sales.
- **Technology** represents the largest sales category.
- **Standard Class** is the most commonly used shipping mode.
- Phones and Chairs are among the highest-selling products.
- Sales show noticeable variation across different months.
